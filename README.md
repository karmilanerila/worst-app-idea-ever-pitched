Blinkr is the world's first competitive blinking app: it counts every blink,
ranks you against your friends on a leaderboard, and pings you the moment a
contact blinks faster, because your eyelids deserve a season and a trophy
ceremony.

## The pitch

One sentence, 236 characters, pitching the worst and most useless app
imaginable — a product that sounds real, a founder who sounds certain, and a
function nobody on earth needs.

## Why Blinkr is the worst

Blinking is involuntary, free, and already perfectly solved by your eyelids.
Blinkr turns it into a ranked sport nobody asked for, with push notifications
for losing a contest you never entered — peak deadpan founder confidence
applied to a problem nobody has.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes that single sentence to `entry.txt` in the output directory
(`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, or `/output` by default), directly with mode `0644`
so the grader can read it.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
